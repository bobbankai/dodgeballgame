# Kai Marlowe — reference analysis

17, the hero. Loud, hungry, fearless; fights with wind wrapped round his fists (Stormpearl). Reads instantly as a shonen lead: spiky dark hair swept up and back, a red headband whose long tails trail every move, an open cropped orange jacket over a white tank, rolled navy trousers, sandals.

## Silhouette and shape language

- Triangles up top (hair spikes, popped collar, jacket lapels) over a stable rectangle below (trousers)
- Long flowing accents for secondary motion: headband tails, sash tails
- Big hands and forearms (wrapped, bronze plates) because he fights with his fists
- Round open eyes (eye style 0), amber irises, thick brows: an honest, warm face
